Check out new paper presentinu the performance and scientific potential of the UPFLOW experiment. The experiment achieved an impressive 91% data completeness, delivering high-quality broadband seismic recordings. Beyond recording earthquakes and imaging Earth's deep interior, the data capture an extraordinary diversity of signals, from Earth's free oscillations to whale calls, opening exciting opportunities for research spanning solid Earth geophysics, oceanography and marine biology.
