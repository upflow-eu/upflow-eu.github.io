UPFLOW was featured in a BBC News article by science correspondent Jonathan Amos, bringing the experiment's discoveries to a global audience. The article highlighted how our network of ocean-bottom seismometers detected exciting signals from the 2022 Hunga Tonga volcanic eruption, alongside an extraordinary variety of recordings from earthquakes to whale calls.
